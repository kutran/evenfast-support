EvenFast Privacy Policy

Updated November 17, 2026

EvenFast is a personal fasting timer. The app stores fasting sessions, goals, labels, schedules and preferences on your device. EvenFast does not send this information to a developer-operated server.

Information in the app

No EvenFast account is required. The current app has no developer-operated analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. The developer does not collect your fasting records, location, contacts or payment-card details through the app.

Apple services and subscriptions

Apple processes subscriptions and payments. EvenFast uses StoreKit on your device to check subscription status, introductory-offer eligibility and restored purchases. Apple handles your Apple Account and payment information under its own policies. Apple may also process device backups, App Store activity or diagnostics according to your Apple settings and agreements. See Apple's privacy information.

Notifications, widgets and Live Activities

If you allow notifications, EvenFast schedules reminders locally. Reminder text, widgets and Live Activities may display fasting information on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. You can control these surfaces in iOS settings. The app and its widget extension share local state through an Apple App Group.

Your choices and local data

You can disable or delete repeating schedules and change notification permission in iOS settings. You can remove the app and its local data using iOS storage controls. Device backups may retain earlier copies according to your backup settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store account settings.

Support outside the app

You can contact the developer through the EvenFast public support tracker. GitHub requires its own account for posting and processes information under its privacy policy. Information you post there is public and may be seen by the developer and other visitors. Do not post fasting records, health information, purchase receipts, passwords or verification codes. This optional support channel is separate from the app.

Changes

This policy will be updated if EvenFast's data handling changes. The current version and its update date will be available on this page.
