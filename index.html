EvenFast Support

EvenFast is a personal fasting timer with optional Pro repeating schedules and local-time reminders.

Get help

Report a general app problem or ask a question in the public support tracker. Posting requires a GitHub account. Include your iOS version, app version and the steps that caused the issue. Do not post health information, fasting history, purchase receipts or Apple Account credentials.

Subscriptions and trials

Manage or cancel your subscription in your App Store account settings. Deleting EvenFast does not cancel a subscription. Eligible customers can receive one introductory trial across the Pro subscription group; changing plans does not provide a second trial. Apple's purchase confirmation shows the actual price, trial eligibility and renewal terms for your account.

Reminders

Enable notifications for EvenFast in iOS settings. Reminders follow your local time and ask you to open the app; they do not start a fast automatically. Disable or delete a schedule in the Schedules tab to stop its repeating reminders.

Privacy

Read the EvenFast Privacy Policy for details about local storage and Apple services.
